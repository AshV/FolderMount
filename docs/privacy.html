Privacy Policy for FolderMount

Last updated: September 15, 2026

FolderMount is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding user data.

1. No Personal Data Collection

FolderMount is a completely offline desktop utility. It does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) or user data.

2. No Telemetry or Analytics

FolderMount does not contain any analytics SDKs, telemetry frameworks, tracking cookies, or third-party monitoring services. No usage statistics or error reports are transmitted over the internet.

3. Local Device Storage Only

FolderMount operates entirely on your local machine:

  • Startup Settings: FolderMount registers a standard Windows registry entry under your user profile (HKCU) to restart the app and re-mount drives when you log in (enabled by default; can be disabled in Settings at any time).
  • Application Settings: General configuration (such as your startup preference) is kept locally at %APPDATA%\FolderMount\settings.json.

4. Network Access

FolderMount does not require, request, or initiate any internet connections. It functions completely without an active network connection.

5. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, the updated version will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date.

6. Contact

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: https://github.com/AshV/FolderMount.
